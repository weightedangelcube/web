(you contain multitudes?)\
dissatisfied!\
it is hard\
to write about (fight the influence)\
when you ask\
whether it was right all along\
or maybe it’s inside you\
and maybe you’re the evil one\
machinery, factories you can't hide\
like a wild weed, uprooted\
speak my culture like a slur\
words, concentric circles, spiral\
type your sentences\
(speak the singular truth)\
you win, if-then\
I pledge my allegiance\
and kill the girl inside of me—\
amen.
