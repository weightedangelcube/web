watching your eyes paint a symphony\
I wonder if you can hear me too\
[lost in the darkness above and below]

wish you were here\
wish I could fly,\
into your\
arms

[surely you would hold me]\
as the\
world burnt away\
from my mistakes

we can just\
[be friends]\
two runaways\
and an impossible dream

so far apart\
[and yet, we are connected]\
[for eternity]
