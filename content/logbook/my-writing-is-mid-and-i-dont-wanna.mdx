because all I do nowadays is read other poems. watch\
authors of a different feather glide ink upon paper create masterpieces,\
draw half-stars half-moons half-lidded beauty and observe\
my hands, my raw knobbed burnt swollen bone flesh skin and think about\
all the things I haven't done yet. I picked up the pen\
and drew the outline of my suffering into the paper\
jagged shards and broken constellations; thinking\
maybe if I arranged them into a sentence perfect enough, they would\
give me the answer I wanted, the secret I needed to face the person\
reflected at me in the words.
