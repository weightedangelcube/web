dear blonde girl—\
you woke up one day with everything I wanted,\
and I hated you.\
I'm sorry\
for trying to steal\
for trying to make you feel like less\
for trying to replace you.\
eight years later\
you were in my chemistry class\
quiet, absorbed\
and I would think—\
lonely\
and I was too absorbed in my guilt to care.\
in another universe\
we are friends\
and you would forgive me for the pain I caused you—
\
let me forgive me for the pain I caused you.
