half the poems on my page\
are dedicated to you,\
(were, before I privated them)\
so it’s weird to be writing about you again.\
it’s been three years—\
years of snow and sun and life happening,\
and if I’m to be honest, I’m kinda confused. I\
don’t know why you cut me off like that\
don’t know why I’m mad that you cut me off (like that)\
and if I’m honest, I’m thankful you were there.\
think I was happy for a while,\
but things are different now (I think)\
and—\
I hope I wasn’t (too) weird.\
I hope I’m a happy memory for you too,\
(I know I’m probably not)\
but thank you, anyways—\
I’m gonna try and move on now, too—\
and see you later
