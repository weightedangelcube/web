I know;\
oh my god, I know—\
I threw up on you and asked you to clean up the mess—\
and naturally, the consequence is mines. covering my\
mouth now won't do you any good, and I suppose it's\
simply a formality to say I'm sorry,\
I'm sorry—\
—I'm sorry,\
I'm so, so sorry. I wanted you to get to know the\
new me—I really thought I had healed\
and then proved myself wrong with this\
mess I've made: there's no changing what's\
already been done. I wish it wasn't like this\
but it's not right to take your hand and\
lead you into the dark—you deserve better,\
better, better than I could be. oh—\
is it over? I'll never get a chance to\
thank you for saving my life. this is\
goodbye,\
goodbye,\
goodbye—

\
<small>You know where to find me. If you ever want to talk again, I'll wait for you.</small>
