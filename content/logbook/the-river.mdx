_(i wonder why you left but at the same time i know)_\
why am I like this? (that's a stupid question)\
I didn't want to hurt you (too selfish not to)\
too hard to not fuck up,\
delicate heart in my hands, why did I touch it?\
I mean,\
you didn't have anyone else,\
I didn't know what it meant—\
and I wanted you to stay close.\
now these demons will chase me around—\
concentric circles in my mind—\
I avoided you for so long,\
and our mess has left a permanent stain on everything it touched.\
I'm sorry—\
I know you're softer than you let on,\
(you can't fool me after all that)\
and I thought I would bring ruin to the altar.\
so now that I've brought ruin to the altar—\
_(you're coming back, right?)_\
will you forgive me?
