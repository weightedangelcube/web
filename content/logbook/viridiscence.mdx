I break out the mold—\
sunshine and azure and fields of weathered green\
and it's springtime.\
time for freedom, irresponsible\
time to reach for the beyond, and miss\
and laugh about it.\
\
you know, last spring was weird,\
and so was the spring before that,\
_(and the one before that)_\
so no doubt this one'll be weird in hindsight as well.\
~~we've~~ done a lot of stupid things\
but it's okay!\
\
we'll enjoy it as it happens\
forget about it afterwards\
feel the guilt twist its way inwards\
fade away into the clouds, and return—\
ready\
\
to do it again.
