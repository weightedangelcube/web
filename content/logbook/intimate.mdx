and when I say I'm happy for you,\
I truly mean it—\
I'm glad,\
but I don't really have any more words.\
finding myself back in the spot, (in circles)\
left behind\
and hurting\
and fighting all these demons—wars, unrequited\
anger and shame and an incessant frustration\
asking,\
never responding\
as you're so close but so far,\
parallel universes I cannot reach—\
I get a little mad.
