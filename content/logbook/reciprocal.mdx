autonomy is a meaningless word\
for no one is truly autonomous\
everyone needs a shoulder to cry on

and yet\
days pass\
meaningless, just like this word

am I searching for you? not necessarily.\
I am waiting for a feeling, a sign\
a signal from heart to heart\
a touch from me to you\
something to fill this empty excuse of a soul

I wonder\
if I’ll ever find you\
I wonder\
if I’ve already found you

wires strung to a burnt-out lightbulb\
a call that was never returned\
yet the electricity flows through for eternity\
shining red copper, like love\
that will never be requited

my fault\
for leaving a crack\
in my heart\
for someone else to fill
