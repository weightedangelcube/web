because I'm grieving you and it's my fault.\
I'm mourning, spilled over your corpse while the hilt\
of the offending weapon burns in my hand. yet\
in cold, blue reality you're not even dead: just gone, left\
without a wayward glance.\
so I push that same blade into the heart of my own,\
pressing the memory into my chest to see if it\
brings you back. it won't. who am I\
to complain? I don't ask for things I don't\
deserve. you thought you could get\
away with it. I thought I could get away with it—\
I'm sure now you've realized you\
can do whatever you want,\
so why can't I?
