it’s strange\
the snow, the cold, the silence.\
no one seems to be concerned with\
how many more sunrises will you no longer be able to see?\
I did not know him, and yet\
the ache is deep-set,\
a remnant of memories gone by.\
and it was me, once, if I had not been so stubborn\
so I wonder if I could’ve saved you,\
if I hadn’t been too selfish to think of you\
futile—\
and yet the winter lingers,\
stray regrets of a forsaken past.

<small>I hope the next world treats you better than this world did. fly high, brother</small>
